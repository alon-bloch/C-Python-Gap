PARTITIONING VISUALIZATION (Quick Sort - Distance)
================================================

Initial Array: [Charlie (800), Dan (500), Alice (200)]
Addresses: 0x1000, 0x1040, 0x1080

Step 0: Setup
-------------
* pivot   points to 0x1080 (Alice: 200) - This is our benchmark!
* small   points to 0x1000 (Charlie)
* current points to 0x1000 (Charlie)

Step 1: current = 0x1000 (Charlie: 800)
---------------------------------------
Action: Compare current (800) vs pivot (200).
Result: 800 > 200 -> NO SWAP.
small   remains at 0x1000.
current moves to 0x1040.

Step 2: current = 0x1040 (Dan: 500)
---------------------------------------
Action: Compare current (500) vs pivot (200).
Result: 500 > 200 -> NO SWAP.
small   remains at 0x1000.
current moves to 0x1080. (Loop Ends)

Step 3: Final Pivot Swap
------------------------
The loop is done. Now we must put the pivot (Alice) into its correct place.
Action: Swap data at *small (0x1000: Charlie) with data at *pivot (0x1080: Alice).

[Memory View After Final Swap]:
0x1000: Alice (200)   <-- small (Correctly placed!)
0x1040: Dan (500)
0x1080: Charlie (800) <-- former pivot location

Return Value: Pointer to 0x1000.

Summary of Logic:
-----------------
The 'current' pointer scanned the array looking for anything smaller than the pivot.
The 'small' pointer waited at the front to receive those smaller elements.
Since everything was larger than Alice, 'small' never moved, and Alice was 
swapped into the very first position at the end.
